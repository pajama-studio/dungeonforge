# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Criticise work, not the person who made it. Assume the
other person is trying.

## What that means here

Dungeonforge is a technical project and technical disagreement is welcome —
this codebase was built by measuring things and being willing to say "that
didn't work." Say so about code freely. What is not welcome:

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private, including unwelcome attention
  after being asked to stop.
- Discrimination or derogatory remarks about who someone is — their race,
  gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability,
  appearance, body size, age, nationality, or religion.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments, and
anywhere else someone is representing the project.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately to the maintainers by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/pajama-studio/dungeonforge/security/advisories/new)
on this repository — despite the name, it is the confidential maintainers-only
channel this project has, and a conduct report sent there will be read as one
and kept private.

If the behaviour also breaks GitHub's own rules, you can and should
[report it to GitHub](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) as well; you do
not have to wait for us.

Reports are handled by the maintainers, who will not act on a report about
themselves. We will respond within a week.

## What we will do

Depending on what happened: a private word, a request for a public apology, an
edit or removal of the offending content, a temporary block, or a permanent
ban from the project's spaces. We will tell the reporter what we decided.

Maintainers who do not act in good faith on this document should be held to it
by the same means as anyone else.
